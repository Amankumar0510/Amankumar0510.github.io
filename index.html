04 · Interactive lab
Balance the robot with PID

      
Change the gains, then push the robot. This is a simplified browser simulation of an inverted pendulum, not data from the physical robot.

    

      

        
        
Balancing

      

      

        
        
Proportional: reacts to how far it leans. Too low and it falls.

        
        
Integral: removes a small lean that stays over time.

        
        
Derivative: damps the wobble. Try setting it to 0.
